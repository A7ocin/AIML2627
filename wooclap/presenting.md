# Presenting the 16-lecture course

Open the desired lecture from the course home page. Each part has its own slides, handout, previous/next links, references and wrap-up.

The 92 existing questions are split between the lecture parts without changing their Wooclap URLs. Sign into Wooclap in your presenting browser, then use **Open live question** or **Open in Wooclap**. Wooclap controls starting the question, voting and results. If embedded sign-in is blocked, use the separate-tab link. **Continue lecture** returns to the deck; leaving a slide unloads its iframe but does not itself close voting.

Existing events are reused for the two halves of each original pack. The home page numbers lectures 01–16; question IDs keep their original prefixes so that existing links remain stable. Publish the entire AIML directory, including the shared assets in the original eight directories.
