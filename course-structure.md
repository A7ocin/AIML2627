# Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning — 16-lecture structure

Each lecture has a separate slide deck and full-text handout. Boundaries follow topic transitions and balance demonstrations, discussion and derivations; slide counts are not used as a proxy for teaching time alone. Original material and all 70 interactive examples are retained. New openings, recaps, takeaways and references make each part usable in one sitting.

Classification Parts 1 and 2 divide after exploring logistic probabilities, before likelihood-based fitting. Parts 3 and 4 divide after linear SVMs, before nonlinear feature maps: the shorter fourth part contains the denser kernel derivation. Regression divides after diagnosing underfitting/overfitting, before the bias–variance and inference material. Clustering divides after K-means and Gaussian mixtures, before hierarchical clustering and PCA.

Old slide and handout addresses redirect to the appropriate new part, including original slide/chapter bookmarks and Wooclap question bookmarks. The old asset directories remain in place because the new decks share their images, libraries and interactive engines. Preserve them when publishing.

The eight existing Wooclap events are reused, with questions placed in the appropriate new lecture. Stable L00–L07 question IDs refer to the original banks; the instructor guide identifies the current lecture. No live questions need re-importing.
