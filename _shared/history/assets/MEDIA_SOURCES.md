## Playback

Open `00_History/index.html` normally to present the deck. From a local `file://` page, Play opens the corresponding YouTube watch page. When the deck is served over HTTP, Play loads an embedded player; changing slides removes the player and restores the preview. Each video also has a permanent watch link. Videos need an Internet connection. The rendered gallery and PDF show static previews.
